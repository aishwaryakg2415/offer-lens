    This score estimates risk from patterns in the text and public records. It is not a finding that any person or company is committing fraud. Legitimate employers sometimes write poorly and careful scammers sometimes write well, so use the result as a prompt to verify.

    Methodology Offer Lens evaluates about 40 text signals across 11 risk categories, plus domain registration age, mail-server records and community reports. Signals combine as independent evidence: score = 1 − ∏(1 − weight). Indicators of a genuine hiring process reduce the score by up to 40%. Any request for payment, an OTP, or the movement of money sets a minimum score of 50. With a Claude review, the final score is 55% rules and 45% Claude.
